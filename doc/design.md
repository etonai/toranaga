# Toranaga-Class Cruiser

## Concept Design Document (v0.1)

### Overview

The **Toranaga-class Cruiser** is a unique Domain-era prototype designed as the ultimate autonomous expeditionary warship. It is intended to serve as the player's flagship for an entire campaign, combining overwhelming combat capability with long-range exploration and self-sufficiency.

Unlike conventional cruisers or capital ships, the Toranaga represents a different design philosophy. Rather than scaling up hull size, Domain engineers concentrated their most advanced reactor, shield, sensor, and computational technologies into a cruiser-sized platform.

Only a single prototype was ever completed before the Collapse.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Toranaga is intentionally overpowered.

This is not intended to be a balanced multiplayer or faction ship.

The purpose of the design is to answer the question:

> *"If the Domain built the greatest warship it possibly could, what would it look like?"*

The player begins a new campaign commanding this ship alone.

It should remain relevant—and desirable—for the entire game.

---

# Core Identity

**Role**

* Autonomous Expeditionary Flagship
* Fleet Killer
* Deep Space Explorer
* Long-Term Independent Operations
* Technology Recovery Platform

The Toranaga should feel capable of operating for years without support.

---

# Combat Philosophy

The Toranaga wins battles through technological superiority rather than brute durability.

It should feel:

* Extremely responsive
* Exceptionally efficient
* Precise
* Fast
* Difficult to overwhelm

The player should feel like they are piloting an advanced prototype rather than simply a larger ship.

---

# Intended Gameplay

The Toranaga should be capable of:

* Defeating multiple vanilla capital ships
* Exploring the entire Sector without dedicated logistics support
* Serving as the player's permanent flagship
* Remaining enjoyable to pilot throughout an entire campaign

---

# Desired Characteristics

## Mobility

Exceptional for its size.

Target feel:

* Cruiser-sized hull
* Destroyer-like acceleration
* Excellent turn rate
* High maximum speed

The Toranaga should be capable of repositioning rapidly and exploiting enemy weaknesses.

---

## Shield System

Primary defensive system.

Desired characteristics:

* Omnidirectional shield
* Extremely high efficiency
* Low upkeep
* Excellent unfolding speed

The ship survives by preventing damage rather than absorbing it.

---

## Flux System

One of the defining features.

Goals:

* Massive flux capacity
* Exceptional dissipation
* Capable of sustained combat
* Rarely forced to vent under normal engagements

Flux management should still matter, but the ship should reward aggressive, skilled piloting.

---

## Armor

Strong, but secondary.

Armor should protect against mistakes rather than serve as the primary defense.

---

## Hull Integrity

High enough to survive major engagements, but not absurdly inflated.

The Toranaga should never feel like a simple hit point sponge.

---

# Weapon Philosophy

The Toranaga is intended to possess firepower exceeding that of vanilla capital ships while retaining cruiser mobility.

Desired characteristics:

* Multiple large weapon mounts
* Broad firing arcs
* Excellent point defense coverage
* Flexible weapon loadouts

The ship should support a wide variety of builds without forcing a single optimal configuration.

---

# Exploration Capability

Exploration is a primary mission profile.

The Toranaga should comfortably replace an Apogee during exploration.

Desired built-in capabilities include:

* Surveying Equipment
* Efficiency Overhaul
* Solar Shielding

Additional campaign bonuses may include:

* Reduced supply consumption
* Increased fuel efficiency
* Improved sensor performance
* Improved salvage capability

---

# Campaign Logistics

The Toranaga should be capable of serving as a self-contained expedition vessel.

Desired capabilities:

* Large fuel reserves
* Significant cargo capacity
* Sufficient crew accommodations
* Low operating costs relative to its performance

The player should not feel compelled to immediately acquire freighters or tankers.

---

# Design Philosophy: Integrated Systems

The Toranaga should possess numerous built-in technologies that consume no ordnance points.

These represent integrated Domain engineering rather than aftermarket modifications.

Examples:

* Surveying Equipment
* Efficiency Overhaul
* Solar Shielding
* Resistant Flux Conduits
* Advanced Turret Gyros

---

# Visual Style

General appearance:

* Sleek
* High-tech
* Elegant
* Purpose-built
* Minimal unnecessary ornamentation

The silhouette should communicate speed and precision rather than brute force.

The vessel should look advanced even compared to existing high-tech ships.

---

# Lore

Late in the Domain's expansion, strategic doctrine shifted.

Rather than constructing increasingly massive capital ships, engineers explored the concept of autonomous strategic cruisers capable of independently exploring, surveying, securing, and reclaiming frontier systems.

The Toranaga project represented the culmination of this effort.

Its objectives included:

* Long-duration autonomous deployment
* Fleet superiority
* Scientific survey
* Archaeological recovery
* Deep-space exploration
* Independent command capability

Only one prototype successfully completed trials.

Before production could begin, the Collapse ended the project.

The Toranaga vanished.

Its fate became one of the many unsolved mysteries of the Domain.

---

# Campaign Role

The Toranaga should encourage a different style of campaign.

Instead of assembling a fleet because it is required, the player assembles a fleet because it is strategically advantageous.

The flagship remains central throughout the game rather than eventually being replaced.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Toranaga should not merely be stronger.

It should feel **better engineered**.

Every system should communicate the same idea:

> This is what happens when the greatest civilization in human history builds a single ship without compromise.
