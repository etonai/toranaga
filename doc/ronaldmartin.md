# Ronald Martin-Class Exploration Cruiser

## Concept Design Document (v0.1)

### Overview

The **Ronald Martin-class Exploration Cruiser** is a Domain-era long-range exploration vessel engineered to venture farther into unknown space than any previous manned expedition. Designed to operate independently for years at a time, it combines state-of-the-art scientific equipment with overwhelming defensive capabilities.

Unlike conventional exploration ships, the Ronald Martin was built on a simple premise:

> **An expedition that cannot defend itself cannot complete its mission.**

Rather than relying on military escorts, the Ronald Martin was designed to survive—and prevail—against whatever threats the unexplored Sector might contain.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Ronald Martin is an autonomous exploration platform.

Every system aboard the vessel exists to maximize its ability to discover, study, and return with knowledge from the frontier.

Combat is not its primary purpose.

However, when threatened, it possesses sufficient firepower and defensive capability to defeat virtually any hostile force encountered during exploration.

---

# Primary Mission

The Ronald Martin was designed for:

* Deep-space exploration
* Planetary survey operations
* Scientific research
* Archaeological investigation
* Domain technology recovery
* First-contact reconnaissance
* Long-duration autonomous deployment

The vessel is expected to remain beyond established territory for years without logistical support.

---

# Operational Philosophy

The Ronald Martin follows a philosophy of **prepared exploration**.

Rather than avoiding danger, it is designed to investigate dangerous regions directly.

Its sensors locate the unknown.

Its laboratories analyze it.

Its weapons ensure it survives long enough to bring the discoveries home.

---

# Exploration Systems

The Ronald Martin carries the most advanced exploration systems developed by the Domain.

Built-in capabilities include:

* Surveying Equipment
* Advanced Sensor Array
* Deep-Space Sensor Suite
* High-resolution Planetary Imaging
* Long-range Spectroscopic Analysis
* Solar Shielding
* Efficiency Overhaul
* Advanced Salvage Systems
* Extended Navigation Computer
* Autonomous Research Facilities

The ship is intended to be the benchmark against which all other exploration vessels are measured.

---

# Scientific Facilities

The cruiser contains extensive onboard facilities, including:

* Geological laboratories
* Biological containment facilities
* Archaeological research bays
* Engineering workshops
* Materials analysis laboratories
* Advanced computational centers

The vessel is capable of conducting comprehensive scientific investigations without returning to civilization.

---

# Combat Philosophy

Although exploration defines its mission, the Ronald Martin is engineered to survive hostile encounters independently.

Its combat doctrine emphasizes:

* Superior shield technology
* Exceptional flux efficiency
* Long-duration engagements
* Precision firepower
* High survivability

Enemy fleets should quickly discover that attacking an exploration cruiser was a catastrophic mistake.

---

# Mobility

The Ronald Martin maintains respectable cruiser mobility despite its extensive scientific infrastructure.

Desired characteristics:

* Good top speed
* Strong maneuverability
* Efficient acceleration
* Excellent handling for a vessel of its size

The ship must be capable of navigating hazardous environments as effectively as it maneuvers through combat.

---

# Shield System

The primary defensive system.

Characteristics:

* Omnidirectional shield
* Extremely high efficiency
* Low upkeep
* Rapid deployment
* High resistance to sustained fire

Its shields are intended to preserve both the crew and irreplaceable scientific equipment.

---

# Flux System

A defining technological advantage.

Goals:

* Massive flux reserves
* Exceptional dissipation
* Sustained weapon operation
* Outstanding shield endurance

The ship should remain combat effective even during prolonged engagements.

---

# Armor and Hull

While shielding remains the primary defense, the Ronald Martin incorporates reinforced structural protection to safeguard critical research facilities.

Characteristics:

* Heavy cruiser-grade armor
* Excellent hull integrity
* Extensive internal compartmentalization
* Redundant life-support systems

The ship is engineered to survive damage that would force most exploration vessels to abandon their mission.

---

# Weapon Philosophy

The Ronald Martin is equipped with a weapons package rivaling dedicated warships.

Suggested characteristics:

* Multiple Large weapon mounts
* Several Medium weapon mounts
* Extensive Point Defense network
* Broad firing arcs
* Flexible loadout options

Although intended for defense, its combat capability exceeds that of most frontline cruisers and rivals many capital ships.

---

# Campaign Characteristics

The Ronald Martin is designed to support extended independent expeditions.

Desired capabilities include:

* Large cargo capacity
* Extensive fuel reserves
* Reduced supply consumption
* High salvage efficiency
* Superior sensor strength
* Increased sensor range
* Improved navigation performance
* Excellent combat readiness recovery

A single Ronald Martin should be capable of exploring vast regions of the Sector without requiring dedicated support vessels.

---

# Visual Style

The Ronald Martin should project confidence rather than aggression.

Design characteristics:

* Elegant high-tech hull
* Large integrated sensor arrays
* Prominent communications structures
* Flush-mounted weapon systems
* Powerful but efficient engine clusters
* Distinctive exploration equipment integrated into the hull

Its silhouette should immediately communicate that this is an exploration vessel—one that just happens to be extraordinarily dangerous.

---

# Lore

As Domain expansion pushed ever farther into unexplored regions of space, conventional survey ships proved increasingly vulnerable to pirates, autonomous defense systems, hostile remnants, and unknown threats.

Rather than assigning large military escorts to every expedition, the Domain commissioned a new class of exploration cruiser capable of defending itself while carrying the finest scientific equipment ever installed aboard a starship.

The Ronald Martin program embodied the belief that exploration and survival were inseparable.

Although enormously expensive, these cruisers dramatically expanded humanity's understanding of the frontier before the Collapse ended further production.

---

# Gameplay Identity

The Toranaga dominates through military supremacy.

The Ronald Martin dominates through scientific supremacy backed by military excellence.

Where other exploration ships avoid conflict, the Ronald Martin simply continues its mission.

Its crew expects to encounter danger.

Its designers ensured they would survive it.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Ronald Martin should never feel like a warship with survey equipment bolted on.

Nor should it feel like a fragile research vessel.

It should embody the ideal of a true exploration cruiser: a ship capable of crossing the unknown, uncovering humanity's greatest discoveries, and defending those discoveries against any force that would prevent them from reaching home.
