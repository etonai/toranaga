# RayChen-Class Frigate

## Concept Design Document (v0.1)

### Overview

The **RayChen-class Frigate** is the smallest vessel developed under the same advanced Domain weapons program that produced the Toranaga-class Cruiser and Nebula-class Destroyer.

Designed as a rapid-response combat platform, the RayChen pushes frigate technology to its absolute theoretical limits. It sacrifices endurance, logistics, and versatility in favor of unmatched speed, precision, and combat efficiency.

While the Toranaga commands battles and the Nebula dominates engagements, the RayChen controls the pace of combat itself.

---

# Design Philosophy

The RayChen is the ultimate frigate.

Its purpose is not to survive through overwhelming durability, but through absolute tactical superiority.

It should feel like:

* Faster
* Smarter
* More responsive
* More efficient

than any other frigate ever built.

---

# Primary Mission

The RayChen was designed for:

* Fleet screening
* Interception
* Pursuit
* Escort elimination
* Flanking attacks
* Hunter-killer operations

Every system emphasizes rapid offensive action.

---

# Relationship to the Family

The RayChen shares the same engineering philosophy as the Toranaga and Nebula.

Common technologies include:

* High-efficiency shield generators
* Advanced flux reactors
* Integrated fire-control systems
* Precision thrusters
* Advanced sensor fusion
* Shared visual architecture

It is unmistakably a member of the same family of warships.

---

# Combat Philosophy

The RayChen should never feel fragile.

Instead, it should feel almost impossible to pin down.

Enemy ships should struggle to keep weapons on target long enough to threaten it.

Victory comes through movement, positioning, and relentless pressure.

---

# Mobility

The defining characteristic.

Desired feel:

* Extremely high maximum speed
* Near-instant acceleration
* Exceptional deceleration
* Outstanding turn rate
* Extremely responsive strafing

Piloting should feel effortless.

---

# Shield System

Primary defense.

Characteristics:

* Omnidirectional shield
* Extremely efficient
* Minimal upkeep
* Instant deployment

The RayChen should be able to raise and lower shields fluidly during combat.

---

# Flux System

Another defining characteristic.

Goals:

* Excellent dissipation
* High efficiency
* Continuous firing capability

The player should rarely feel limited by flux management while flying skillfully.

---

# Armor

Light but advanced.

Armor exists only to absorb occasional mistakes.

Remaining stationary should always be dangerous.

---

# Hull Integrity

Above average for a frigate.

Enough to survive unexpected heavy hits without becoming a damage sponge.

---

# Weapon Philosophy

The RayChen mirrors the weapon philosophy of its larger siblings while remaining appropriate for a frigate hull.

Suggested layout:

* 1 Large weapon mount
* 2 Medium weapon mounts
* 4 Small weapon mounts

The emphasis is on overlapping firing arcs and exceptional forward offensive capability.

The ship should be capable of threatening destroyers while remaining dangerous to cruisers through mobility.

---

# Ship System

The RayChen's system should reinforce its role as an interceptor.

Potential concepts include:

* Burst Maneuver Drive
* Combat Blink
* Vector Thrusters
* Precision Engagement Matrix
* Flux Surge

The system should reward aggressive positioning rather than retreat.

---

# Campaign Characteristics

Unlike the Toranaga, the RayChen includes no exploration enhancements.

It possesses only standard frigate logistics.

Its sole purpose is combat excellence.

---

# Visual Style

The RayChen should clearly resemble the Toranaga and Nebula.

Shared characteristics include:

* Sleek, angular hull plating
* Integrated weapon housings
* Compact bridge design
* High-output engine clusters
* Clean, purposeful lines

Despite its small size, it should immediately project advanced technology.

---

# Lore

The RayChen project sought to determine the upper limit of frigate performance.

Engineers were instructed to ignore manufacturing cost, maintenance complexity, and production scalability.

The resulting vessel incorporated technologies previously reserved for experimental cruisers, producing a frigate capable of outperforming ships many times its displacement.

Although enormously successful during trials, its extraordinary production requirements prevented mass manufacture before the Collapse.

Only a handful of prototypes are believed to have existed.

---

# Gameplay Identity

The Toranaga controls the battle.

The Nebula breaks the enemy line.

The RayChen hunts anything that tries to escape.

Flying the RayChen should feel like fencing with a rapier rather than swinging a hammer.

---

# Guiding Principle

The RayChen should never feel like a larger ship compressed into a frigate hull.

It should feel like the absolute realization of what a frigate can become when every engineering compromise is removed.

It is not the smallest member of the family.

It is the fastest, most agile, and most precise.
