# Nebula-Class Destroyer

## Concept Design Document (v0.1)

### Overview

The **Nebula-class Destroyer** is an advanced Domain-era destroyer designed under the same strategic development program as the Toranaga-class Cruiser.

While the Toranaga was intended to serve as an autonomous expeditionary flagship, the Nebula was conceived as a fleet superiority escort—a compact warship capable of defeating ships far larger than itself through overwhelming technological advantage.

Like the Toranaga, the Nebula was designed without compromise. Every system emphasizes combat effectiveness rather than cost or ease of production.

Visual inspiration: `inspiration/scalartech-solutions-0.9.3b/graphics/tahlan/ships/tahlan_tress.png`
---

# Design Philosophy

The Nebula is not intended to be balanced against vanilla destroyers.

Instead, it represents the absolute pinnacle of destroyer design.

Its intended role is to answer the question:

> *"What if the Domain built the greatest destroyer it could possibly engineer?"*

The Nebula should comfortably outperform even many vanilla cruisers while remaining distinctly smaller than the Toranaga.

---

# Relationship to the Toranaga

The Nebula and Toranaga share a common technological lineage.

Shared characteristics include:

* Advanced shield technology
* High-output flux reactors
* Exceptional maneuverability
* High weapon density
* Sophisticated fire-control systems
* Identical visual design language
* Similar hull construction techniques

The Nebula should immediately feel like a member of the same family.

---

# Primary Mission

The Nebula was designed for:

* Fleet escort
* Fleet interception
* Heavy strike missions
* Destroying enemy cruisers
* Screening against smaller craft
* Independent patrol operations

Unlike the Toranaga, exploration and logistics were never design priorities.

Every subsystem exists to improve combat performance.

---

# Combat Philosophy

The Nebula wins through speed, precision, and relentless offensive pressure.

It should feel:

* Extremely agile
* Highly responsive
* Aggressive
* Technically sophisticated

Piloting the Nebula should reward initiative and constant movement.

---

# Mobility

Outstanding.

Target feel:

* Frigate-like acceleration
* Extremely fast turning
* High top speed
* Rapid strafing

The ship should be capable of outmaneuvering almost anything larger than itself.

---

# Shield System

Primary defense.

Characteristics:

* Omnidirectional shield
* Extremely efficient
* Fast deployment
* Low upkeep

The Nebula survives through mobility and superior shielding rather than armor.

---

# Flux System

A defining feature.

Goals:

* Exceptional dissipation
* Large flux reserves
* Continuous offensive capability

The ship should rarely be forced to disengage because of flux limitations.

---

# Armor

Moderately strong.

Enough to forgive mistakes, but insufficient to encourage careless piloting.

---

# Hull Integrity

Excellent for a destroyer.

Capable of surviving prolonged engagements against larger opponents.

---

# Weapon Philosophy

The Nebula intentionally mirrors the Toranaga's overall layout, scaled appropriately for a destroyer hull.

The emphasis is on broad firing arcs and flexible loadouts.

Possible layout:

* 2 Large weapon mounts
* 2 Medium weapon mounts
* 6 Small weapon mounts

Strong point-defense coverage should allow the ship to remain effective while closing on larger targets.

---

# Ship System

The Nebula's system should emphasize offensive momentum.

Potential concepts include:

* Reactor Overdrive
* Advanced Maneuver Thrusters
* Flux Amplifier
* Precision Targeting Matrix

Whatever system is selected should encourage aggressive engagements rather than defensive play.

---

# Campaign Characteristics

Unlike the Toranaga, the Nebula carries no specialized exploration equipment.

It possesses:

* Standard fuel capacity
* Standard cargo capacity
* Standard survey capability

Its only purpose is winning battles.

---

# Visual Style

The Nebula should clearly resemble the Toranaga.

Shared characteristics:

* Smooth armored surfaces
* High-tech architecture
* Clean weapon integration
* Advanced engine geometry
* Similar bridge design

When viewed together, they should appear to belong to the same production family.

---

# Lore

As the Toranaga program matured, Domain planners recognized that even the most capable flagship required escorts capable of matching its speed and combat effectiveness.

The Nebula project adapted many of the same technologies into a destroyer-sized platform.

The resulting vessel dramatically exceeded the performance of conventional destroyers and was capable of engaging cruisers with confidence.

Production remained extremely limited due to the extraordinary cost and manufacturing complexity of its advanced reactor systems.

The Collapse ended the program before fleet-wide deployment.

---

# Gameplay Identity

The Toranaga dominates the battlefield through overwhelming strategic presence.

The Nebula dominates through tactical superiority.

It is smaller.

It is faster.

It is more aggressive.

Yet it carries enough firepower to make even capital ships respect it.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Nebula should not feel like a conventional destroyer with better numbers.

It should feel like a glimpse of the next generation of warship design—a vessel engineered without compromise, built to stand beside the Toranaga as one of the Domain's finest creations.
